Privacy Policy

Salty St Kitts: The Guide · Last updated October 6, 2026

Salty St Kitts: The Guide ("the app") is made by Salty St Kitts. We keep it simple: we don't want your personal information and we don't collect it.

What we don't collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We don't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or photos. We don't use your location. We don't use advertising, ad tracking, or analytics tools, and we don't sell or share any data.

What stays on your phone

To work offline, the app saves a copy of the guide's content (stops, photos, tips) on your device. Whether you've unlocked the full guide is also saved on your device. You can remove all of this at any time by deleting the app.

Purchases

Purchases in the iPhone app are handled by Apple through the App Store. Purchases on our website are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Those payments are covered by Apple's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy.

Outside services

When you have signal, the app downloads its latest content from our server, map images from Google Maps, and the St Kitts weather forecast from Open-Meteo. Like any website, these services may see standard technical information such as your IP address so they can send the content to you. Tapping "Directions" opens Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
